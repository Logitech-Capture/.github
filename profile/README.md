## Why This Exists

Most camera passes scatter the preview, the take, and the project folder. **Logitech Capture** keeps the camera you own and the file you just made on one PC.

Logitech Capture solves **one specific problem**: recording a shot you mean to keep. Open Logitech Capture Software, check the frame, and let Logitech Recording Software write the take beside the project.

If you are tired of clips that do not match the shot you saw, **Logitech Capture** is for you.
